# Walkthrough agenda: choose a usable beginner route

Prepared by **Gage Plott**. November 3, 2026 review proposal. The instructor has acknowledged receiving

## Agreed starting point

Assume zero Python experience. Use live demonstrations that students repeat on
their laptops, with complete worked examples and guided discussion. There is no
grading, evaluation, assignment, or submitted work. Software assertions check
the material; they are not student assessments.

## Proposed route to walk through

1. **Day 1, Python/Qiskit:** open and run cells; numbers, text, lists, imports,
   and function calls; one-qubit X/H circuits, samples, histograms; save/restart.
2. **Day 2, Grover:** two qubits and one target; compare zero, one, and two
   iterations (target probabilities 1/4, 1, 1/4), then change the target together.
3. **Day 3, QFT/QPE:** see a two-qubit complex phase pattern and inverse-QFT
   recovery; prepare a target eigenstate and decode three-bit phases 3/8 and 1/8.

Use 75 minutes of activity plus 10 minutes of margin in each 85-minute period.
The **two-day fallback** is Day 1 plus Grover. If phase estimation is preferred,
it replaces Grover as the single algorithm day. If Shor is essential, agree a
brief compiled-N=15 substitution and identify what it replaces; do not append
it to an already full session. HHL remains supporting material.
